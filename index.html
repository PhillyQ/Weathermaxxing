Where the numbers come from

    Weather, air quality and history: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Alerts, written forecasts, station readings and outlooks: the U.S. National Weather Service and NOAA. Sunrise, sunset and twilight are calculated in the page from NOAA’s solar equations; moon position uses SunCalc; season and moon-phase dates are precomputed. Forecasts are model output. For anything safety-critical, go by the National Weather Service.
